Ephyra privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Ephyra app for Android phones, tablets and Android TV (application ID nl.midasvo.findroid.ce, formerly Findroid CE). Ephyra is a community fork of Findroid, published by midasvo.

What we collect

Nothing. Ephyra does not collect, store or share any personal information with the developer or with third parties. It has no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

Your Jellyfin server

Ephyra is a client for Jellyfin, a media server that you or someone you know runs. The app only talks to the servers you add yourself. To sign you in and play your media it sends your user name, password, playback progress and device name to that server. That server is under the control of whoever runs it, not of the Ephyra developer, and its own privacy practices apply.

Data on your device

Your server addresses, sign-in tokens, settings and downloaded media are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Permissions

  • Internet and local network: to reach your Jellyfin server.
  • Notifications: to show download progress and playback controls.
  • Foreground services: to keep downloads and playback running when the app is in the background.

Children

Ephyra is not directed at children.

Changes

Changes to this policy take effect when they are posted on this page.
