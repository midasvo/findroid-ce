Findroid CE is now Ephyra. Same app, new name and jellyfish icon. The update installs over Findroid CE and keeps your login, settings and downloads.

- The app is called Ephyra across phone and Android TV.
- Release APKs are named ephyra-v<version>-libre-<abi>.apk.
- Device profiles report as Ephyra Device Profile.
- Moving forward, Ephyra uses its own versioning starting at v2.0.0.
