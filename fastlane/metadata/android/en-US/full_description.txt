Findroid CE is a community-driven fork of Findroid, the native Jellyfin client for Android. It provides a native user interface to browse and play movies and series from your Jellyfin server.

To use this app you must have a Jellyfin server.

What Findroid CE adds:
- Resumable download engine with SD card and quality selection
- Redesigned downloads screen, bulk downloads, and Smart Downloads
- Dolby Vision direct play and improved audio codec handling (Atmos, ffmpeg fallback)
- Video quality selector and version picker during playback
- Subtitle styling (colors, size, fonts) and wide format support (PGS, VobSub, SSA/ASS)
- Per-series audio and subtitle track memory
- Chapter list and media segment skipping (intro/outro)
- Background audio playback
- Library enhancements including hide-watched filter and auto offline mode

Based on Findroid by Jarne Demeulemeester.

Thanks for using Findroid CE!
